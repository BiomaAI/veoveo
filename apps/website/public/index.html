Veoveo is a self-hosted autonomy harness for physical AI, where humans, robots and agents learn, play and evolve together in simulated and real worlds.

What Veoveo does

  • Always-on agents. They wake when a watched condition changes, act, and pick up long-running tasks again after a restart.
  • Robots and simulators on one platform. Agents rehearse in simulation and command robots in the field through the same controls.
  • Onboard intelligence. Agents run on the robot itself and keep working when the network drops.
  • Multi-modal perception. Agents fuse every sensor stream an operation produces into one model of the world and reason across all of it at once, on the customer's own GPUs.
  • One replayable record. Sensor streams, video, decisions and actions share one timeline that humans and agents replay and query.
  • Policy outside the model. Identity, policy and audit checks run outside the AI model on every action.
